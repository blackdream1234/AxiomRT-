Deliberate test faults

These inject intentional faults to demonstrate containment and recovery. They are marked DELIBERATE TEST FAULT in this console; results appear only in the serial log and events — success is never assumed.

Guided demonstration sequence

Follow in order; watch the serial console and the Faults/Events pages after each step.

  1. Confirm the system is alive: tasks, net status.
  2. Normal operation: run hello, then ls and storage info.
  3. Synthetic network: net send-test, then net stats.
  4. Authority: caps, then run fault_demo — its IPC attempt is denied (CAP_DENIED) and its fault is contained.
  5. Survival: tasks — unrelated services keep running.
  6. Driver containment: driver fault block, observe with drivers, recover with driver restart block.
  7. Network driver containment: net fault, observe net status (driver_down), recover with net restart, confirm counters reset with net stats.
  8. Normal operation again: run hello.
  9. Finish on the Verification page.
